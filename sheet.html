Auspice & Moon Gift
Choose an Auspice and Tribe on Main.
Gibbous Moon
Auspice Ability: Prophetic Dreams
The Storyteller may grant prophetic dreams; roll Intelligence + Occult to interpret. After a night of 4+ hours' sleep, once you may declare an action or event was foretold: you or a packmate gain +3 to one action, or a Storyteller character takes -3.
Auspice Skills: Crafts, Expression, Persuasion · Renown: Glory · Gift affinities: Inspiration, Knowledge, Gibbous Moon
Half Moon
Auspice Ability: Darkness Into Light
Once per chapter, cure a werewolf of Death Rage (the target is Stunned for the next turn if in Basu-Im), or force one into Death Rage with Presence + Empathy + Primal Urge vs Resolve + Composure (works only on Uratha).
Auspice Skills: Empathy, Investigation, Politics · Renown: Honor · Gift affinities: Insight, Warding, Half Moon
New Moon
Auspice Ability: Closer Than You Thought
Once per chapter, choose one: move your Initiative to within 1 of a target's; close to use teeth/claws on one opponent not attacking you; or reduce Doors needed in Social maneuvering by 2.
Auspice Skills: Larceny, Stealth, Subterfuge · Renown: Cunning · Gift affinities: Evasion, Stealth, New Moon
Crescent Moon
Auspice Ability: Spirit Howl
Once per chapter, spend 1 Essence (no roll): a howl silent in the Flesh echoes through the Shadow. Lower-Rank spirits flee or hide, more powerful ones show deference; hostile spirits' Defense is penalised by your Wisdom.
Auspice Skills: Animal Ken, Medicine, Occult · Renown: Wisdom · Gift affinities: Elemental, Shaping, Crescent Moon
Full Moon
Auspice Ability: Tenacious
Once per chapter, ignore the effects of any Conditions or Tilts hindering you in combat for two turns (they are not resolved).
Auspice Skills: Brawl, Intimidation, Survival · Renown: Purity · Gift affinities: Dominance, Strength, Full Moon
